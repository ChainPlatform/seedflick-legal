Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Game data

Your best score, language setting and purchase status are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that collect or store this data. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

2. Advertising

Seed Flick uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

4. Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
